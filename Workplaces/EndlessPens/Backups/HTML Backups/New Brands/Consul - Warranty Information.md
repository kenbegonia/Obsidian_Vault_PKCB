# Warranty Information
---
Consul writing instruments are covered by a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship.

Warranty cases may include, for example, a defective nib, problems with threaded parts, ink leakage caused by a manufacturing defect, or other defects in materials or workmanship.

Normal wear, accidental damage, misuse, unauthorized modifications or repairs, damage caused by unsuitable inks, and natural variations in the color or pattern of the material are not covered by the warranty.

If a customer reports a possible warranty issue, please contact Consul at info@consulpen.com with a description of the problem, and, whenever possible, photos or a short video showing the issue. If they confirm that the issue is covered by the warranty, the defective pen or part will be replaced free of charge.
