# Shopify Spring 2026 Edition: Key Announcements & Features
**Theme:** "Everywhere"  
**Date Analyzed:** June 2026

---

### 🤖 Agentic Commerce & AI Integration
* **Universal Commerce Protocol:** Standardizes backend product data automatically so autonomous AI agents can read, recommend, and checkout products natively inside AI chats.
* **The "Agentic Plan":** Allows non-Shopify sites to sync inventory directly to Shopify Catalog to unlock distribution across AI channels.
* **Sidekick App Integration:** Deep-links Shopify's AI assistant with ecosystem apps (Klaviyo, Loop, etc.) to trigger complex multi-app admin tasks via chat.
* **Sidekick Multi-Tasking & Formats:** Executes long admin tasks in the background while enabling quick voice-lookup business queries directly from an Apple Watch.
* **Shopify Inbox AI Associate:** Tailors store-front chat recommendations using a signed-in customer’s centralized Shop purchase history.
* **SimGym:** Simulates artificial shopper behaviors on any storefront theme to identify hidden conversion and design bottlenecks.

---

### 📈 Storefront, Marketing & Merchandising
* **Campaign Autopilot:** Outsources cross-channel marketing optimization to an AI manager that self-corrects budget allocation within your guardrails.
* **Shop Campaigns Expansion:** Connects ad placement programmatically to high-intent conversational channels like ChatGPT, Pinterest, and Microsoft Monetize.
* **Rollouts (Native A/B Testing):** Introduces secure native scheduling and A/B testing for storefront themes, checkout flows, and account configurations before going live.
* **Variant-Level Publishing:** Grants absolute administrative control to restrict or publish specific product variants per sales channel or geographic market without workarounds.
* **Shopify Smart Pricing App:** Delivers real-time first-party pricing recommendations derived from inventory velocity, supplier costs, and seasonality data.
* **Democratized B2B Features:** Lowers the entry barrier by giving Basic, Grow, and Advanced plans access to company profiles and up to three B2B catalogs.

---

### 🏪 Retail Hardware & Shopify POS
* **Shopify POS v11:** Fully rebuilds point-of-sale architecture around an always-present cart to shave customer creation and checkout times down to under a minute.
* **Verifone Victa Mobile Terminal:** Replaces legacy retail setups with a proprietary, dual-purpose handheld barcode scanner and countertop payment terminal.
* **Unified Returns & Exchanges:** Integrates refunds, exchanges, and new additions smoothly within a single point-of-sale checkout screen transaction.
* **Multi-Entity Retail (Plus):** Empowers enterprise brands to coordinate distinct legal corporate entities operating separate physical storefronts inside a single country dashboard.
* **Scannable POS Discounts:** Distributes admin-generated QR discount codes that retail staff can scan directly using a POS device camera.

---

### ⚙️ Operations, Infrastructure & Identity
* **365-Day Customer Sessions:** Keeps returning storefront shoppers authenticated securely for an entire year to maximize returning customer retention.
* **Identity Provider Syncing:** Maps profile fields and user tags directly between Shopify and enterprise authentication suites like Auth0, Ping Identity, and Azure ID.
* **Automated Vaulted Payments:** Integrates Shopify Flow to automatically execute vaulted B2B corporate card payments immediately upon order fulfillment or invoice due dates.
