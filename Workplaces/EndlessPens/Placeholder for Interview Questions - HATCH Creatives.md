# 1.

```
For stationery it started way back in my childhood, where there was one stationery store just on the first floor of our apartment. I would visit there everyday after school and spend most of my allowances collecting different colors of the Pilot Hi-tec-c and Uniball Signo pens. And several decades later, I then encountered fountain pens and inks when someone else gifted me a Benu Talisman(Four leaf clover), and I immediately bought another Kaweco(Iguana Blue) right after that. These pens all come in my favorited color families, and in order to match a perfect ink with those pens, I fell right into the next rabbit hole of the endless ink color options!
```

# 5.

```
I always suggest the Pilot Kakuno! It is very beginner friendly but also provides a really smooth writing experience. Especially the transparent ones where you could see how fountain pens work, or trouble-shoot when something's wrong with the ink flow, like when shimmering particles get piled up inside the pen and block the flow.
```

# Bio

```
Authan Chen is a Taiwanese illustrator who creates fantasy and surrealism art in both traditional and digital medium. The artist-run shop provides a variety of whimsical stationery and handmade products that varies from art prints, rubber stamps, tote bags, hand-sewn ornaments, enamel pins, and paper goods. You may find his art greatly inspired by queer pop culture, botanical illustrations, mythology, and also his obsession with axolotls! By using pastel colors and sinuous shapes to depict surrealistic scenes, he aims to bring these dreamlike fantasies to reality.
```
